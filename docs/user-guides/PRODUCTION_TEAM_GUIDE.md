# Production Team (Crew) User Manual

This manual guides production crew members (Camera Operators, Technicians, Audio Engineers, Assistants, and Drivers) on viewing shoot assignments and acknowledging schedules.

---

## 1. Receiving Shoot Assignments
When a shoot is approved by the CMO, assigned crew receive assignments via:
1. **Brevo Email**: Contains full shoot details, reporting time, rolling time, venue address, and Google Maps link.
2. **MSG91 Voice Call**: For consenting staff, an automated call from the Sri Sankara TV Production Desk recites your assignment and prompts for spoken or keypad acknowledgement.
3. **Calendar Invite**: Synchronized directly to your work calendar.

---

## 2. Portal Acknowledgement
1. Log in to the production portal on your mobile device or laptop.
2. The dashboard displays **"My Shoot Assignments"**.
3. Inspect your assignment briefing (reporting time, venue, role).
4. Click **"✓ Acknowledge Assignment"** to confirm your attendance.
5. If you have an urgent conflict, click **"Report Scheduling Issue"** and enter a note. Your Production Head will be alerted immediately.
