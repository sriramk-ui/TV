# Accounts Department User Manual

This manual guides the Accounts and Finance team on accessing approved Production Approval Forms (PAFs), verifying budgets, and exporting financial documentation.

---

## 1. Accessing Approved PAF Records
1. Log in with your Accounts credentials (`accounts@srisankaratv.com`).
2. Navigate to **"PAF & Budgets"**.
3. In accordance with channel governance, only **Approved PAFs** appear in your ledger. Pending, draft, or returned PAFs are strictly concealed until authorized by the CMO.

---

## 2. Reviewing & Exporting PAFs
1. Click **"View PAF"** on any approved record.
2. The PAF details modal displays:
   - Unique PAF Series Reference (e.g. `PAF-00001`)
   - Associated Program and Executive Producer
   - Complete itemized expenditure breakdown (Particulars, Unit, Quantity, Rate, Amount, Payment Mode)
   - CMO Approval Timestamp and Digital Audit Seal
3. Click **"Print / Save PDF"** to generate an official PDF carrying the required audit header:
   > *"Sri Sankara TV — Approved Estimated Expenditure (Not Proof of Payment)"*
