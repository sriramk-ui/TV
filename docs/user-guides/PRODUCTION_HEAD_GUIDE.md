# Production Head User Manual

This manual guides the Production Head through reviewing EP requests, managing crew and equipment allocations, preventing scheduling collisions, preparing PAF budgets, and submitting approval packages to the CMO.

---

## 1. Reviewing Incoming Shoot Requests
1. Log in with your Production Head credentials.
2. In the **Overview** or **Shoot Schedule** section, filter by status `requested`.
3. Select any shoot to inspect requirements, venue, reporting timings, and requested equipment.

---

## 2. Assigning Crew & Conflict Resolution
1. Click **"Assign Crew"** on the target shoot occurrence.
2. For each day of the shoot:
   - Select camera operators, sound technicians, assistants, driver, and vehicle.
3. The system executes real-time availability checks:
   - If an employee is already assigned to another shoot on that date within overlapping hours (including travel buffers), a red collision warning appears:
     > *"Collision: V. Karthik is assigned to Shoot #SSTV-0002 (06:00 - 13:00)."*
   - You must reassign an available team member before submitting.

---

## 3. Preparing the Production Approval Form (PAF)
1. Navigate to **"PAF & Budgets"** and click **"New PAF"** (or click **"Prepare PAF"** from the shoot card).
2. Select the linked EP shoot request. The show name, dates, venue, and EP name are populated automatically.
3. Add itemized expense rows:
   - **Particulars**: e.g., *Camera Kit Rental*, *Diesel for OB Van*, *Breakfast & Lunch for 8 crew*, *Toll charges*.
   - **Unit**: e.g., *Days*, *Vehicles*, *Persons*.
   - **Quantity**: Numeric quantity.
   - **Unit Rate (₹)**: Rate per unit.
   - **Payment Mode**: *Cash*, *UPI*, or *Bank Transfer*.
4. The system calculates line totals and grand estimated total automatically using decimal-accurate arithmetic.
5. Click **"Save PAF"**.

---

## 4. Submitting Combined Package to CMO
1. Once both crew assignments and the PAF are ready, click **"Submit Schedule + PAF for CMO Approval"**.
2. The package is validated and forwarded to the CMO. An initial alert and hourly pending reminder schedule are activated.
